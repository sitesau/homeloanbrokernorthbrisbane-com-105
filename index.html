Who each quote angle suits

Borrower groups should not be treated as interchangeable, because each creates different documents, timing pressure and lender policy questions.

  • First home buyers use the review to test deposit gaps, discuss the First Home Owner Grant and the federal First Home Guarantee, and check whether lenders mortgage insurance is likely to arise.
  • Refinancers ask for the current rate, fees and structure to be compared against available alternatives, then decide whether switching is worth the discharge process.
  • Investors ask about interest-only, offset, SMSF and multiple-property structures only where those topics match their strategy.
  • Construction borrowers clarify how the lender wants the build contract, progress payments and settlement sequence handled.

Inputs that change the number

The strongest use of a free review is to arrive with clean inputs, because the broker confirms borrowing power, lender shortlist and a realistic timeline from the goal, deposit and income supplied. Prepare the details that move serviceability before asking for a figure.

  • Income: salary, overtime, bonuses, business income or other income a lender may assess differently.
  • Commitments: credit cards, car loans, personal loans, dependants and living costs.
  • Deposit: savings, gift, equity, grant discussion or a guarantor possibility if relevant.
  • Property goal: owner-occupier purchase, refinance, investment purchase, or a construction or renovation split.
  • Timing: contract date, finance clause, settlement target or refinance discharge expectations.

A borrowing power calculator is a screening estimate only. A precise number requires a live serviceability assessment, so treat calculator output as a starting filter, not a lending decision.

Fees, commission and disclosure checks

Before committing, ask for the Credit Quote and the Credit Proposal Disclosure. These should make clear who is paid, by whom and when, with lender commission disclosed in writing ahead of commitment.

Going straight to an existing bank can be efficient for a simple profile that wants one lender answer. Broker help becomes more useful where lender comparison, a second opinion on structure, or settlement coordination is needed. For refinance reviews, the broker compares the current rate, fees and structure, then handles the application, chases approvals and remains available for annual rate reviews after settlement.

Those are service process claims, not guarantees of approval or savings. Credit applications still depend on lender approval and lending criteria, and ASIC Moneysmart frames a home loan as a decision about features, repayments, comparison and suitability rather than headline rate alone.

Timing from contract to settlement

Two planning windows matter. A standard purchase commonly allows 30 to 45 days from accepted contract to settlement. A refinance usually settles in 4 to 8 weeks, depending on the outgoing lender discharge process.

Those windows guide planning, but each borrower still needs to check the contract, conveyancer timetable and lender requirements on their own file. Confirm the lender shortlist before application lodgement, confirm the expected valuation, approval and document signing sequence, and ask whether any refinance discharge step could delay settlement. Common questions

Is a 30 minute pre-approval review a lending decision? No. It is a screening step that confirms borrowing power, a lender shortlist and a realistic timeline. A precise number requires a live serviceability assessment, and approval always depends on lender criteria.

Does the borrower pay the broker directly? No direct broker fee is charged under the typical offer. The broker is paid commission by the lender when the loan settles, and that commission is disclosed in writing before the borrower commits.

How long should settlement planning allow? Plan around 30 to 45 days from accepted contract to settlement on a standard purchase, and 4 to 8 weeks for a refinance depending on the outgoing lender discharge process, then confirm the contract, conveyancer and lender timetable for your own file.
